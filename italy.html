Classic Pizza Margherita — simple, fresh, and iconic.

← Back to cookbook index

Ingredients (makes 2 medium pizzas)

  • 500 g (about 4 cups) strong bread flour
  • 325 ml warm water
  • 7 g instant yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200 g canned San Marzano tomatoes, crushed
  • 125–150 g fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra-virgin olive oil

Method

  1. In a bowl, mix warm water and yeast; let sit 5 minutes until slightly foamy.
  2. Combine flour and salt in a large bowl. Add yeast mixture and mix to form a rough dough.
  3. Turn onto a floured surface and knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise 1–2 hours until doubled.
  4. Preheat oven to the hottest setting (250–275°C / 475–525°F) with a pizza stone or inverted baking tray inside.
  5. Divide dough into two balls. On a floured surface, stretch one ball into a thin round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
  6. Spread a thin layer of crushed tomatoes (season with a pinch of salt) leaving the rim free. Add pieces of mozzarella and a drizzle of olive oil.
  7. Bake on the hot stone/tray 6–10 minutes until crust is blistered and cheese melted. Remove and scatter fresh basil leaves before serving.

Notes

Use fresh ingredients: good-quality canned tomatoes and fresh mozzarella make a big difference. For authentic flavor, bake at a very high temperature and serve immediately.

Tips & Variations

For a chewier crust use higher-protein bread flour; try a cold overnight ferment in the fridge for deeper flavor. Substitute burrata for mozzarella for a richer pizza.
