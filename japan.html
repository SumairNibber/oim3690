Simple Maki Sushi — sushi rice and basic roll technique.

← Back to cookbook index

Ingredients (makes ~4 rolls)

  • 2 cups sushi rice (short-grain)
  • 2 1/4 cups water
  • 60 ml rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 nori sheets
  • Fillings: cucumber (julienned), avocado slices, smoked salmon or cooked tuna, or imitation crab
  • Soy sauce, pickled ginger, wasabi (to serve)

Method

  1. Rinse rice under cold water until water runs clear. Drain and combine with 2 1/4 cups water in a pot. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook 12–15 minutes. Let rest 10 minutes off heat.
  2. Warm rice vinegar with sugar and salt until dissolved. Fold into cooked rice using a rice paddle or wooden spoon; fan rice while folding to cool slightly.
  3. Place a bamboo mat on the counter, lay a sheet of nori shiny-side down. Wet hands and spread a thin layer of rice over the nori leaving a 2 cm border at the top.
  4. Arrange fillings in a line near the bottom edge. Lift the mat and roll tightly away from you, pressing gently. Seal the edge with a little water.
  5. Slice roll into 6–8 pieces with a sharp wet knife. Serve with soy, ginger, and wasabi.

Notes

Sushi can be customized with many fillings. For a more authentic experience, use sashimi-grade fish and keep tools and hands very clean.

Tips & Variations

Cool the rice to room temperature before rolling. Wet your knife between cuts to get clean slices. Try cucumber+avocado for a vegetarian roll, or add a thin smear of mayo mixed with sriracha for heat.
