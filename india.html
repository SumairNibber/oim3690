Chicken Biryani — fragrant layered rice and spiced chicken.

← Back to cookbook index

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 500 g basmati rice
  • 600 g chicken pieces (bone-in or boneless)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp biryani masala (or garam masala)
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili powder
  • Whole spices: 4 cardamom pods, 4 cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick
  • Fresh cilantro and mint, lemon, oil or ghee, salt

Method (simplified)

  1. Rinse rice and soak 20–30 minutes. Par-cook rice in boiling salted water for ~6 minutes; drain and set aside.
  2. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili, biryani masala, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  3. In a heavy pot, heat oil/ghee. Fry sliced onions until golden; remove half for garnish. Add whole spices, then add marinated chicken and cook until chicken is nearly done and oil separates.
  4. Layer half the par-cooked rice over the chicken. Sprinkle some fried onions, chopped cilantro, mint, and a squeeze of lemon. Add remaining rice and repeat garnishes.
  5. Cover tightly and cook on very low heat (dum) for 20–25 minutes until flavors meld. Let rest 10 minutes, then gently fluff and serve with raita or salad.

Notes

Biryani has many regional variations. This simplified method produces fragrant layered rice and tender spiced chicken — adjust spices to taste and try saffron-infused milk for an extra aroma.

Tips & Variations

Toast whole spices briefly before frying to deepen flavor. For a richer biryani, use ghee and a pinch of saffron soaked in warm milk. Rest the cooked biryani covered for 10 minutes before serving.
